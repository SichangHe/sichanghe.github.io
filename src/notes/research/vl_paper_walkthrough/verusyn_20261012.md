# Reducing the Costs of Proof Synthesis on Rust Systems by Scaling Up a Seed Training Set
(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)
Tag: `di2026reducing`, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04910)

VeruSyn: cheap Verus proof generator by fine-tuning on 6.9M synthetic verified programs
- 6.9M programs: 5.7M direct, 1.2M after debugging (sec. 1, p. 2)
iterative self-synthesis
- fine-tune on SAFE seed proofs, generate Rust + spec + proof from a prefix only, dedupe, run verus, self-debug, retrain on all prior data
  - stops when 95% of latest 500 programs are duplicates (sec. 4.1, p. 5)
  - alone gives narrow, shallow verus features (sec. 1)
  - inference: synthetic distribution may diverge from real systems
tutorial-based coverage
- experts write about 1,000 seeds covering every Verus Tutorial knowledge point
  - 2,000 programs per seed (about 2M), add seeds for weak features, then 4,000 per seed (about 5.8M)
  - keyword coverage from SAFE's 4 knowledge points to 12, all 20 hit at least once
  - limits: repetition around tutorial examples; keywords miss semantic coverage
long-CoT agent trajectories
- Sonnet 4.5 agent on 289 VeruSAGE tasks, keep successful trajectories
  - 4,557 CoTs combined with part 1; covers 15/20 features
  - 871 of 2,000 sampled synthetic programs proved by Sonnet in one try
  - eval excludes the 289 tasks to avoid leakage
results (Accuracy@K = at least one verified output in K samples, 5 debug rounds)
- Qwen2.5-Coder-32B VeruSyn vs Sonnet 4.5
  - VerusBench (150 tasks, AutoVerus's count; SAFE's has 139): 83% vs 76%
  - VeruSAGE-Bench (560 tasks): 49% vs 54%
    - 289 training tasks excluded; 44 Anvil Controller + 3 Atmosphere too long, scored 0
    - not comparable to VeruSAGE paper's 81% (849 tasks, agentic CLI)
- cost: "only 2% of the cost" (sec. 1, p. 2)
  - no debugging $0.17/task vs over $8 for Sonnet (47x), 39% accuracy
  - with debugging $0.61 vs over $30, 49%
  - beats o4-mini at 4% of its cost
