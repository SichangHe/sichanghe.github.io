# DRIFT: Decompose, Retrieve, Illustrate, then Formalize Theorems(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)Tag: `zhang2025drift`, [paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10815v4)## purpose and resultDRIFT is a four-stage retrieval-augmented autoformalization pipeline: decompose an informal theorem into concept queries, retrieve formal dependencies, illustrate their use with existing theorems, and formalize the original statement. The paper’s abstract says: “We introduce DRIFT, a novel framework that enables LLMs to decompose informal mathematical statements into smaller, more tractable ‘sub-components’.” It evaluates Lean 4 on ProofNet (374 theorems), MiniF2F-test (224 after removing 20 noncompiling or duplicate examples), and ConNF (961 research-level theorems). On ConNF, GPT-4.1 with DRIFT reaches BEq+@10 62.33%, versus 6.76% zero-shot and 20.08% DPR; DeepSeek-V3.1 reaches 54.21% versus 11.03% zero-shot (Table 2, PDF p.8).
