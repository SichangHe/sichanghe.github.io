# Lean-STaR: Learning to Interleave Thinking and Proving
(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)
Tag: `lin2024leanstar`, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10040)

lean-star: 7B prover writes a short natural-language thought before each lean tactic, then self-trains on lean-checked proofs
- arXiv:2407.10040v5, ICLR 2025, lean 4 math only; verus forms are inference
- lean alone decides success, thoughts never checked and may be wrong
  - thoughts are a search aid with no evidential weight
retrospective rationale generation
- build (state, thought, tactic) from (state, tactic) pairs
  - show gpt-4 the state and the true tactic, ask for a forward reason
  - forward prompting without the tactic works poorly even for gpt-4
- anti-hindsight prompt rules
  - at most 2 sentences, start from the goal, never name the tactic or its lemmas
- scale: 17,256 trajectories, 52,438 steps annotated
- verus form: remove one annotation (assert, invariant, proof fn call), error as state, annotation as tactic
  - single removals may leave states where the annotation is not the natural next step
thought-augmented prediction (lean-cot)
- sft 1 epoch on 231,240 (state, tactic) pairs, then fine-tune on thought triples
- only the tactic goes to lean
- miniF2F-test (244), InternLM2-7b: sft 29.5% to lean-cot 32.8%
  - 80 vs 72 problems, single run, no variance
expert iteration (lean-star)
- sample K=32 per problem at T=1.0, keep only lean-accepted trajectories, dedupe, up to 3 per problem
- iteration 1: 34.0%, iteration 2: 34.8%, K=64: 36.1%
  - gains shrink, +1.2 then +0.8
  - collecting D1 took about 4 days on 8 A100
- control without thoughts: 30.7% base vs 34.0%
  - gain is not only from expert iteration
- plus model: 45.4% pass@32, 46.3% at K=64
- thoughts add output diversity, so more samples help longer
- only theorems provable within 5 generations enter data (inference: hard theorems contribute nothing)
- verus form needs the filter to reject assume, admit, and changed requires/ensures
  - SAFE already runs a self-training loop for verus
sampling instead of best-first search
- K parallel attempts, resample at the same state when a step is rejected
  - best-first with avg log-prob unreliable once free-text thoughts are generated
- thought models: sampling beats best-first by 5 to 9 points
  - plain sft model: best-first slightly better
- verus step costs a full re-verification
leakage check
- of 112 successes, 7 match reference proofs exactly, 6 of them single-tactic
  - 80.36% of successes distinct from reference
  - bounds memorized proofs, not memorized statements
paper inconsistencies
- abstract's 43.4% to 46.3% pass@64 mislabels the sft baseline, plus base at K=64 is 42.2%
- 45.4% vs 45.5% for same setting across tables
- "significantly" with no significance test
- thought-augmented methods not better in all categories despite the claim
threat: low, indirect
- relevant for the recipe of reasoning-annotated data from any checked-proof corpus
