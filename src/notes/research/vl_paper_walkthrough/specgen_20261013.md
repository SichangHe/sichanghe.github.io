# SpecGen: Automated Generation of Formal Program Specifications via Large Language Models
(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)
Tag: `ma2025specgen`, [paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/icse55347.2025.00129)

two-phase jml spec generation for java: conversation, then mutation
- 279/385 programs get verifiable specs (SV-COMP java + 120-program SpecGenBench)
  - measures verifier acceptance and expert rating, not full intent
- gpt-3.5-turbo-1106, temp 0.4, 4 few-shot examples, openjml, 30 min timeout
conversational generate-verify-repair
- feed openjml failure info into the next prompt, max 10 rounds
- outputs often "already very close to the oracle", motivates mutation over discarding
- Verus: swap in `verus` diagnostics, keep contract in an immutable file
exhaustive local mutation of near-miss specs
- 4 operator classes
  - predicative (forall/exists)
  - logical (&&, ||, implication)
  - comparative (<, <=, ==, offsets like `- 1 <=`)
  - arithmetic (+/-)
- replace each refuted candidate with another from its same-template family
  - terminates since an empty selected set is allowed
- ablation of total 279: 223 w/o comparative, 235 w/o logical, 259 w/o predicative, 262 w/o arithmetic
weighted heuristic selection of variants
- score = sum of times(m) * weight(m), weights negative, fewer mutations first
  - weights: comparative -1, logical -2, arithmetic -4, predicative -4
- vs random replacement, fewer verifier calls
  - SV-COMP 9.41 vs 8.91 (5.30%)
  - SpecGenBench 36.20 vs 28.51 (21.23%)
comparison and quality
- Defects4J subset: specgen 38 programs, 55.20% success; conversation-only 28; 4-shot 20; Daikon 15
- expert rating 4.54 vs oracle 4.83, on 15 programs handled by Houdini, Daikon, SpecGen
limits
- passing verifier does not show spec is strong enough
- timeout or rejection does not mean spec is false (openjml incompleteness)
- SV-COMP data 88.7% loop-free, complex loops underrepresented
