# Content Provenance and the Generated Web

(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)

Literature reviews on tracing where content came from, and on how much of
the web is AI-generated. Unfinished as of 6 Oct 2026 22:30 Los Angeles time:
the Claude usage limit stopped the work early.

Started, partial:

- [image_watermarking](image_watermarking.md): watermarks in AI-generated
    images, audio and video.
- [labeling_rules_and_practice](labeling_rules_and_practice.md): laws and
    platform practice for labeling AI-generated content.

Not written yet: text watermarking, C2PA and cryptographic provenance, camera

Recent papers I opened and would start from:

- [How Much of the Internet Is Written With
    AI?](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/),
    Pew Research Center, 2026.
    Its [methodology](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/methodology-ai-content/)
    says "35% of pages in the July 2026 crawl with post-ChatGPT publication
    dates show signs of AI authorship", from 10,000 English pages per Common
    Crawl crawl, scored with an open Pangram detector.
- [The Impact of AI-Generated Text on the
    Internet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26965), Jonas Dolezal, Sawood Alam,
    Mark Graham, Maty Bohacek, arXiv, 2026. Internet Archive sample:
    "by mid-2025, roughly 35% of newly published websites were classified as
    AI-generated or AI-assisted".
- [How Much Is an AI Token Worth? Scaling Laws for Wild AI-Generated Web
    Text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40295), Jenna Russell, Ben Glickenhaus,
    Katherine Thai, John Wieting, Mohit Iyyer, Max Spero, Bradley Emi, arXiv,
    2026. "27.5% of tokens from June 2026 web data are labeled as
    AI-generated by Pangram, rising to 31.1% by August".
- [Verifying Provenance of Digital Media: Why the C2PA Specifications Fall
    Short](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24890), Enis Golaszewski, Neal Krawetz,
    Alan T. Sherman, et al., arXiv, 2026. "the current C2PA specifications
    fail to achieve their claimed security goals".
