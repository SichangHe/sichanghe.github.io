# Review of final allocator memo
(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)

1. **P1 — restrict the claimed public operations to the core allocator.** The memo promises bounds for “allocation and deallocation” ([daily_memo.md:6](/ssd1/sichangheagent/browser-agent-proposal-pipeline-private/runs/2026-10-10/daily_memo.md:6)), but upstream also has `GlobalTlsf`, which acquires pages, and says a lock makes worst-case time depend on contending threads. The memo later excludes “pool growth and global synchronization” ([daily_memo.md:24](/ssd1/sichangheagent/browser-agent-proposal-pipeline-private/runs/2026-10-10/daily_memo.md:24)). State the theorem over the pinned port's core `Tlsf::{allocate,deallocate}` with a supplied pool and no concurrent caller. Otherwise the opening reads as a bound on the user-facing allocator, which the proposal does not cover.

2. **P1 — “reusable accounting” has no testable artifact in this pilot.** The gap is “reusable accounting across unsafe representation boundaries” ([daily_memo.md:20](/ssd1/sichangheagent/browser-agent-proposal-pipeline-private/runs/2026-10-10/daily_memo.md:20)), but every implementation and evaluation step applies only to RLSF ([daily_memo.md:34](/ssd1/sichangheagent/browser-agent-proposal-pipeline-private/runs/2026-10-10/daily_memo.md:34)). One allocator shows a case study, not reuse. Add a second independently structured unsafe component using the interface, or describe the result as an allocator-specific source-event proof architecture.

3. **P2 — qualify “Verus-verified RLSF allocator” at first use.** The evidence is a WIP repository's proof-status table; its build was not reproduced, and its correspondence to upstream RLSF is untested ([daily_memo.md:12](/ssd1/sichangheagent/browser-agent-proposal-pipeline-private/runs/2026-10-10/daily_memo.md:12), [source_cards.md:8](/ssd1/sichangheagent/browser-agent-proposal-pipeline-private/runs/2026-10-10/source_cards.md:8)). Say “the WIP Verus RLSF verification port” in the opening and title.

The source quotations and narrow novelty statement are supported. The verification report is accurate: [budget.rs](/ssd1/sichangheagent/browser-agent-proposal-pipeline-private/runs/2026-10-10/verification/budget.rs:5) verifies only a public scalar token encoding; its successful and negative outputs match the stated results.
